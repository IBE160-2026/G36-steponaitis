# 10 · Coupon Book

**Thesis.** A re-slotting plan is a coupon book: each round tears off in order, and the wallet shows what remains. It refuses the dashboard where finished work vanishes. Applied, kept, outdated and no-space states are stamped on and stay visible.

**Own world.** Jet-age airline ticket wallet. Navy `#14245E`, red `#C4182D`, white coupon stock on paper `#F3F1EB`. Carbon purple `#3F2E8C` for every figure. Archivo Narrow caps for heads, Archivo for UI, Red Hat Mono for codes and numbers. Hairline fare boxes, perforations, double-ruled stamps (APPLIED, KEPT, red VOID, hatched NO SPACE).

**Signature move.** The **route map**: 2,000 location cells as a top-down grid of 9 aisles. Every move in the round is a curved arc from-location → to-location, numbered in move-list order and labelled `R1 · RULE 1 · 4410281`. A coupon stub lists the legs.

**Warehouse layout.** Fill weight shows the class (F solid, M mid, S light, D slashed), and empty cells are outlines. Best locations get a navy frame with a B tab. Dash pattern shows the rule; a filled or ring badge shows the status. Each aisle shows its occupancy %, and the tooltip lists the parts stored.

**Navigation.** A left wallet of standard tabs with a stack of remaining-step stubs, plus a stepper. The features menu is an itinerary: each feature is a leg, and stage 2 is marked STANDBY.

**Risk.** Beyond ~15 arcs the map tangles and needs rule filters. Stamps could tip into costume.

**Trust.** Every move shows its rule, its numbers and its place in the walk. Nothing is silently deleted.
