Ten deliberately different directions, each drawn as SVG at 1440 × 900: warehouse layout and interface first (the two most important surfaces), then landing page, features menu and a sheet of all remaining screens. All data is synthetic. Click any frame to open it full size. "Compare" puts the same surface from all ten concepts side by side.

01

Exploded Parts Catalogue

Thesis. The warehouse is an assembly, and the plan is its exploded view. It rejects the flat heat-map grid with a colour legend.

Own world. White catalogue stock #FFFFFF, line-art ink #15181C (hairlines #BEC4CB), one process blue #0066B0 (tint #E7F0F9) for callouts, selection, primary action. State colour #A3470B only for "no space" and "outdated". Barlow Condensed for labels and figure numbers, Public Sans for body, tabular figures throughout.

Signature move. Fig. 12 shows the warehouse as a 7°/42° dimetric exploded view. Racks A–I lift apart along dash-dot explosion lines. Cells: solid fast-moving, dense hatch medium, sparse hatch slow, stipple dead stock, outline empty. Best locations get a heavier frame and a "B" mark. A recommendation is a dashed blue assembly path from the from-location to the to-location, with a split balloon (ref / rule).

Raise. Hairline discipline: every element snaps to a 1px hairline grid. Registration crosshairs appear only at figure corners. No shadows, no rounded cards.

Navigation. Left "Contents" rail: Plan / Analysis / Recommend / Output, with flow-step numbers 1–6. The features menu is the catalogue's contents page, and stage 2 is listed as "Later edition".

Honest risk. With 2,000 cells at 10px, it is dense. Hatches may blur on poor screens, and the dimetric skew is real geometry work to build.

Why a planner would trust it. Every mark on the drawing keys to a table row that states its rule and its numbers.

02

Orienteering Course

THESIS. A re-slotting plan is a course you walk, not a dashboard you watch.

OWN-WORLD. ISOM orienteering map, light theme. White ground #FFFFFF, margin #F6F4EC, black #161616, yellow #FBE3A0 corridors and #FDF0C6 selection, green #D3ECC6 + hatch #4E9A3A for best locations, brown #9A4E12 for levels and sections. Magenta #A626A4 is used only for moves and recommendations. Type: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next and Mono.

SIGNATURE MOVE. Round-as-course overprint: - start triangle at move 1's from-location - numbered control circles at later from-locations - dotted drop circles at to-locations - solid carry legs tagged with the rule - dotted walk legs - a finish double circle

Rounds toggle as separate courses. The move list prints as the control description sheet.

Warehouse layout. Black rack lines with yellow corridors. Each location is a cell: darker means more picks (solid, ringed, half, slashed), and an open square means empty. Best locations sit in a green, hatch-edged zone. Levels are in brown. A "Walk Path order" bar replaces the scale bar. Occupancy is shown per aisle and in total.

Navigation. A top bar with the plan and data-as-of date, the flow drawn as a course, and a legend-style left nav. "All features" opens a full map legend, with stage 2 shown as "not yet surveyed".

Risk. Long carry legs tangle in big rounds. It needs per-move highlighting and a filter by rule.

Trust. Every line on the map is a move the planner chose, numbered as on the printed list.

03

Visible Record Index

Thesis. Before software, stock was planned on visible-record cabinets. This concept refuses the heat-map grid with a colour legend. Each location is a strip and each stored part a rider, so a misfit is a rider in the wrong column.

Own world. Enamelled steel shell (#2F3B44, #3F4D57, #4E5D68, tray #36434D). Ledger-green card stock (#E5EEE1, #D9E5D4) with black ink #172026. Riders: red #B8322A, blue #2C5AA0, yellow #E3B21C, black #15191C, plain steel #9AA6AE. Public Sans for the UI, Courier Prime only for card data.

Signature move: signal riders. Position on the strip shows the movement class, from Fast on the left to Dead on the right. Shape and colour show the state:

  • Steel bar: stored part
  • Red arrow: recommendation pending
  • Black, punched: keep in place
  • Yellow, cut corner: outdated
  • Blue swallowtail on the label end: best location

Empty locations are dashed strips.

Warehouse layout. Nine drawers side by side, with occupancy on each label holder. Each drawer has sections as rows, levels inside each section and positions across. Pull one out to read full-size strips; hover pops up the record card. A recommendation is drawn as a lifted card moving between drawers.

Navigation. Standard tabs in flow order, each with a step number. The plan menu is a cabinet front with locked stage-2 drawers.

Risk. Overview strips are tiny, so shape cues work only in the pulled-out drawer.

Trust. Every rider sits where its numbers put it.

04

Construction Drawing Set

Thesis. A re-slotting plan is a set of construction drawings: what exists, what is proposed, and a record of every revision. It rejects the dashboard default of cards, KPI tiles and heatmaps.

Own world. Whiteprint sheet #F7F8FC on desk #E7E9F2, blue-violet ink #26296B / #474B8C / #5F64A0, grid #CDD0E6, and one redline #C23B1E reserved for changes. Archivo Narrow caps for labels, Archivo for body text, tabular figures. Sheet borders, grid bubbles, section hatching, title block, section-cut markers.

Signature move: revision clouds and delta triangles. Each location a move touches gets a cloud, and its delta carries the ROUND number. The title block's revision table lists the rounds (△1 Round 1 — 12 moves — 2026-11-11). Dashed = existing or proposed, solid = applied.

Warehouse layout (A-800). A plan with grid bubbles A–I and sections 01–19, every location a cell. F is crosshatched, M hatched, S sparsely hatched, D stippled, and empty cells are blank. Best locations get a heavy outline and a BEST tag. A section cut links to an elevation of aisle A with letter tags. Plus occupancy schedule, key, general notes.

Navigation. A sheet-index sidebar (A-000 to A-700 in flow order, stage 2 as future phase) and a Ctrl+K drawing-index palette where digits jump to sheets.

Honest risk. At small sizes the hatching turns to grey tone, so single locations need zoom.

Why a planner would trust it: every change is clouded, numbered by round and explained, like a signed drawing.

05

Bin Wall

THESIS. The shelf face is the interface. Each aisle looks the way it does from the floor, not like a grid of squares.

OWN-WORLD. Graphite louvre shell (#262A2E) with open slots in #141719. The bin plastics are the movement classes, each with an SVG glyph in a white label window: fast red #D33A2C ▲, medium yellow #F0B323 ◆, slow blue #2462C6 ▼, dead stock grey #8C939A. Green #2C8A55 means only "applied". Light ground #EEEDE8. Fonts: Barlow Semi Condensed for codes, Figtree for body.

SIGNATURE MOVE: the lifted bin. Each aisle is drawn as an elevation of 19 sections × 4 levels × 3 positions. An empty slot holds no bin. Best locations get a white rail or frame and a BEST tag. A recommended move lifts its bin out of the slot (the only drop shadow) with an arrow to a dashed target. Numbered markers link wall and table.

LAYOUT. Nine small walls with occupancy %. Selecting one opens it large, with a tooltip.

NAV. Top toolbar with the plan plate (name, data-as-of date, round) and an 8-step segmented flow. The launcher shows bin-label plates (REC, B/A, MOV…), and stage-2 features are empty slots.

RAISE: the ranked roster. Every part list ranks parts by picks. Each row has a name plate and a one-line "why", e.g. "61 picks · fast-moving · partner of 4410297".

RISK. At full width each bin is 27 px wide. FR-42's red/green occupancy becomes a "Colour by" mode.

TRUST. Every bin is a real location code, and every move shows from, to and why.

06

Floor Marking

Thesis. The tool reads like the floor it changes. It refuses colour-coded pills on a white dashboard.

Own world. Graphite shell #1A1E22, work surface #E9E7E2, sealed-concrete map #B9B6AF. Each tape colour means one thing: yellow #F2C200 for walkways and the primary action, green #23743F for best location, red/white #C0302A for keep in place, blue #1D5DB0 for the route. Type is Overpass, with Saira Stencil One for aisle letters only.

Signature move. ISO 7010 shape grammar: rule 1 is an information square, rule 2 a warning triangle, rule 3 two linked squares. Keep in place is a prohibition circle, outdated a struck-through sign, no space a stop octagon. The same signs appear on the map, the list and the print.

Warehouse layout. A top-down plan with a taped cross walkway. Stencilled aisle letters carry their occupancy %. Each location is a beam-label cell, with levels shown as rows. Class is shown by tone plus a glyph, and an empty cell has a diagonal line. The selected move is a taped arrow along the walkways with its sign at the head.

Navigation. A dark rail holds the plan and data-as-of date. The top bar shows the flow as a walkway. The Directory (G) is a wayfinding board: zones 1–5, arrows for back, here and ahead, and stage 2 marked under construction.

Honest risk. Showing levels as rows makes a dense hybrid, not a literal plan, and signs crowd where recommendations cluster.

Trust: every move shows its rule as a sign and its reason in numbers, on screen and on paper.

07

Network Diagram

Thesis. The flow and the Walk Path are both routes, so both are drawn as transit lines. This refuses the heat-map grid with a stepper on top.

Own world. White #FFFFFF, ink #141414, fare-zone greys #F3F3F0/#E9E9E4. Each line has a colour, a bullet and a stroke: P flow, blue #1C5BB8, solid. W Walk Path, violet #5B2C83. Rule 1, red #D7261E, dashes. Rule 2, orange #E07B00, dots. Rule 3, green #00875A, dash-dot. Stage 2, grey #A3A39C. Type is Hanken Grotesk with tabular figures.

Signature move. The shell's top is a metro line, Import → Move list, with a "You are here" interchange. On the map, pick groups are interchange capsules, and a rule 3 move slides a station along its link into the partner's aisle.

Warehouse layout. The trunk snakes A→I through nine aisle bands. Sections are station ticks. Dots show the state: filled is occupied, ring is empty, double ring is best. Dot size shows the class, and a zoom panel adds F/M/S/D. Moves are dashed transfer lines with rule bullets.

Navigation. The flow line uses standard links, and a compact left list holds the plan's sections. The features menu is a network map with stage 2 on a locked grey line.

Raise: single-size tables. Every data table uses one 13px size. Rank comes only from weight, case, reversal and rules.

Risk. The mirrored rows could be read as the physical layout.

Trust. Every dot is a location code, and every line names its rule.

08

Guide Map

Thesis. The warehouse as a place you find your way around, like a zoo-and-gardens visitor map. It refuses the red/green occupancy grid.

Own world. Flat colour: green #8ED462 land, cream #F5F1E4 water, ink #2C2E2A. Capsules in yellow #F5E211 (primary, best), coral #FF705D (rule 2), blue #2BA0FF (rule 1, you are here) and lilac #EBC1FF (rule 3, stage 2). One face, Bricolage Grotesque 600; display text at 0.95 leading, -0.06em. Motion: 0.4 s with overshoot when a chip crosses or a pin drops.

Signature move. The layout as a guide map. Each aisle is a land lobe holding its rack grid. An empty location is a hole where the water shows through. Class glyphs: square fast, half square medium, dot slow, ring dead stock. Best locations are outlined yellow land with a capsule label on the edge. A move is a dotted route to a capsule pin, with the rule number on its chip. Occupancy % is written on each aisle like a garden name.

Navigation. A slim top bar shows the plan and the data-as-of date. The flow is a row of capsules; on the current step the chip moves to the leading edge. "Map of the app" is the key page, with stage-2 areas as dashed islands marked "Opening later".

Risk. The coastlines and 2,000 glyphs could read as decoration, and the look needs the web font to load.

Trust. Every pin names its rule and numbers; routes are an order, not a distance.

09

Exposure Sheets

THESIS. A plan is a sheet laid over the warehouse, not another screen. Refuses: before/after as two tabs.

OWN-WORLD. A darkroom exposure record on a lit light table. Tones come only from an eleven-step zone ramp, 0 #0D0D0C through V #7E7D78 to X #FAF9F5. One dichroic magenta #D1127F is the "filtration": recommendations and moves only. Legends are Barlow Condensed tracked caps, body is Barlow, and codes and numbers are JetBrains Mono (tabular). Translucent sheets at slight angles, squared inside.

SIGNATURE MOVE. The planning-copy sheet lies over the frozen import sheet. Slide it aside to see the import beneath. Where they differ, magenta marks show the moves: arrows labelled with the rule, a to-location frame, and dashed vacated cells where the import shows through.

WAREHOUSE LAYOUT. Nine aisles drawn as rack elevations (sections 01–19 across, levels 4→1 down, 3 positions). Hatch density shows the movement class: dense for fast-moving, sparse for slow-moving, flat for dead stock. Empty locations are clear paper. Best locations get a B tick and a dark edge. Dotted means keep in place, crossed hatch means outdated or no space, and zone 0 means selected. Occupancy % per aisle (after and before) and for the whole warehouse.

NAVIGATION. Left sidebar with the plan and the numbered flow 1–7. Ctrl K opens the ruled record card listing every feature; stage-2 rows are struck through.

RISK. Hatches in 15 px cells can blur fast-moving and medium-moving together on weak printers.

TRUST. Every mark is the import itself or a magenta move traceable to its rule and numbers.

10

Coupon Book

Thesis. A re-slotting plan is a coupon book: each round tears off in order, and the wallet shows what remains. It refuses the dashboard where finished work vanishes. Applied, kept, outdated and no-space states are stamped on and stay visible.

Own world. Jet-age airline ticket wallet. Navy #14245E, red #C4182D, white coupon stock on paper #F3F1EB. Carbon purple #3F2E8C for every figure. Archivo Narrow caps for heads, Archivo for UI, Red Hat Mono for codes and numbers. Hairline fare boxes, perforations, double-ruled stamps (APPLIED, KEPT, red VOID, hatched NO SPACE).

Signature move. The route map: 2,000 location cells as a top-down grid of 9 aisles. Every move in the round is a curved arc from-location → to-location, numbered in move-list order and labelled R1 · RULE 1 · 4410281. A coupon stub lists the legs.

Warehouse layout. Fill weight shows the class (F solid, M mid, S light, D slashed), and empty cells are outlines. Best locations get a navy frame with a B tab. Dash pattern shows the rule; a filled or ring badge shows the status. Each aisle shows its occupancy %, and the tooltip lists the parts stored.

Navigation. A left wallet of standard tabs with a stack of remaining-step stubs, plus a stepper. The features menu is an itinerary: each feature is a leg, and stage 2 is marked STANDBY.

Risk. Beyond ~15 arcs the map tangles and needs rule filters. Stamps could tip into costume.

Trust. Every move shows its rule, its numbers and its place in the walk. Nothing is silently deleted.
