# 08 Guide Map

**Thesis.** The warehouse as a place you find your way around, like a zoo-and-gardens visitor map. It refuses the red/green occupancy grid.

**Own world.** Flat colour: green `#8ED462` land, cream `#F5F1E4` water, ink `#2C2E2A`. Capsules in yellow `#F5E211` (primary, best), coral `#FF705D` (rule 2), blue `#2BA0FF` (rule 1, you are here) and lilac `#EBC1FF` (rule 3, stage 2). One face, Bricolage Grotesque 600; display text at 0.95 leading, -0.06em. Motion: 0.4 s with overshoot when a chip crosses or a pin drops.

**Signature move.** The layout as a guide map. Each aisle is a land lobe holding its rack grid. An empty location is a hole where the water shows through. Class glyphs: square fast, half square medium, dot slow, ring dead stock. Best locations are outlined yellow land with a capsule label on the edge. A move is a dotted route to a capsule pin, with the rule number on its chip. Occupancy % is written on each aisle like a garden name.

**Navigation.** A slim top bar shows the plan and the data-as-of date. The flow is a row of capsules; on the current step the chip moves to the leading edge. "Map of the app" is the key page, with stage-2 areas as dashed islands marked "Opening later".

**Risk.** The coastlines and 2,000 glyphs could read as decoration, and the look needs the web font to load.

**Trust.** Every pin names its rule and numbers; routes are an order, not a distance.
