# 05 · Bin Wall

**THESIS.** The shelf face is the interface. Each aisle looks the way it does from the floor, not like a grid of squares.

**OWN-WORLD.** Graphite louvre shell (`#262A2E`) with open slots in `#141719`. The bin plastics are the movement classes, each with an SVG glyph in a white label window: fast red `#D33A2C` ▲, medium yellow `#F0B323` ◆, slow blue `#2462C6` ▼, dead stock grey `#8C939A`. Green `#2C8A55` means only "applied". Light ground `#EEEDE8`. Fonts: Barlow Semi Condensed for codes, Figtree for body.

**SIGNATURE MOVE: the lifted bin.** Each aisle is drawn as an elevation of 19 sections × 4 levels × 3 positions. An empty slot holds no bin. Best locations get a white rail or frame and a BEST tag. A recommended move lifts its bin out of the slot (the only drop shadow) with an arrow to a dashed target. Numbered markers link wall and table.

**LAYOUT.** Nine small walls with occupancy %. Selecting one opens it large, with a tooltip.

**NAV.** Top toolbar with the plan plate (name, data-as-of date, round) and an 8-step segmented flow. The launcher shows bin-label plates (REC, B/A, MOV…), and stage-2 features are empty slots.

**RAISE: the ranked roster.** Every part list ranks parts by picks. Each row has a name plate and a one-line "why", e.g. "61 picks · fast-moving · partner of 4410297".

**RISK.** At full width each bin is 27 px wide. FR-42's red/green occupancy becomes a "Colour by" mode.

**TRUST.** Every bin is a real location code, and every move shows from, to and why.
