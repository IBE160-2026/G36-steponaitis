# 01 · Exploded Parts Catalogue

**Thesis.** The warehouse is an assembly, and the plan is its exploded view. It rejects the flat heat-map grid with a colour legend.

**Own world.** White catalogue stock `#FFFFFF`, line-art ink `#15181C` (hairlines `#BEC4CB`), one process blue `#0066B0` (tint `#E7F0F9`) for callouts, selection, primary action. State colour `#A3470B` only for "no space" and "outdated". Barlow Condensed for labels and figure numbers, Public Sans for body, tabular figures throughout.

**Signature move.** Fig. 12 shows the warehouse as a 7°/42° dimetric exploded view. Racks A–I lift apart along dash-dot explosion lines. Cells: solid fast-moving, dense hatch medium, sparse hatch slow, stipple dead stock, outline empty. Best locations get a heavier frame and a "B" mark. A recommendation is a dashed blue assembly path from the from-location to the to-location, with a split balloon (ref / rule).

**Raise.** Hairline discipline: every element snaps to a 1px hairline grid. Registration crosshairs appear only at figure corners. No shadows, no rounded cards.

**Navigation.** Left "Contents" rail: Plan / Analysis / Recommend / Output, with flow-step numbers 1–6. The features menu is the catalogue's contents page, and stage 2 is listed as "Later edition".

**Honest risk.** With 2,000 cells at 10px, it is dense. Hatches may blur on poor screens, and the dimetric skew is real geometry work to build.

**Why a planner would trust it.** Every mark on the drawing keys to a table row that states its rule and its numbers.
