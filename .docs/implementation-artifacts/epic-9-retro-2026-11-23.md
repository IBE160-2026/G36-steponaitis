## Action items

All items are proposals, for the normal dev loop or for Justas. This retrospective applied none of them. Items 1 and 3 should land before Story 16.2 puts the app on a public URL.

1. **[dev] Make the login throttle hold under concurrency.** Check and increment the failure count atomically before bcrypt (a conditional update or a row lock in `store/login_attempts.py`), add a concurrency test that sends more than five parallel wrong logins and expects at most five password checks, and purge `login_attempts` rows whose lock and failures are stale (R1, R7). Remediation.
